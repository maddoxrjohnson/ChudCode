# Phone screen rubric

Each format has its own dimensions, each scored 0 to 4. A call passes when at least
one question was answered and every dimension of its format reaches 2. Each answered
question also gets a score from 0 to 4 and a one-line note; for fundamentals those
scores decide which topics come back for review.

## Recruiter screen

| Dimension   | 0                                  | 2                                                        | 4                                                                                   |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| clarity     | Hard to follow; no thread          | Understandable, with some rambling or backtracking       | Every answer has a clear point, stated first                                        |
| concision   | Minutes per answer, or one word    | Mostly the right length; one answer runs long            | "Tell me about yourself" in 60 to 90 seconds; every answer stops when it is done    |
| specificity | Generic claims ("I'm passionate")  | Some concrete details: a project, a technology, a number | Concrete throughout: what they built, for whom, with what result                    |
| motivation  | No reason for this company or role | A reason that could apply to any company                 | A reason only this company fits, tied to their own experience, plus clean logistics |

## Behavioral screen

| Dimension   | 0                              | 2                                                  | 4                                                                        |
| ----------- | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| structure   | No story, or a hypothetical    | A story with the parts out of order or one missing | Situation, task, action, result, in order, in about two minutes          |
| specificity | "We usually…", no single event | One real event, light on detail                    | One real event with the details that make it believable                  |
| ownership   | Only "we"; their part unclear  | Their part is there but shares the stage           | "I" for what they did, credit given where due, their decisions explained |
| impact      | No result, or "it went well"   | A result, without a number or a lesson             | A measurable result, and what they learned or would do differently       |

## CS fundamentals

| Dimension | 0                                  | 2                                                | 4                                                                 |
| --------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| accuracy  | Most answers wrong                 | Mostly right, with a mistake a follow-up exposes | Right, including the edge cases a follow-up probes                |
| depth     | Definitions only, recited          | Says why, for some answers                       | Says why and when, compares alternatives, and names the trade-off |
| clarity   | Rambling or jargon without meaning | Clear, with some filler or false starts          | Short, structured answers a non-specialist could follow           |

## Delivery

Delivery is not a dimension on its own; it shows up in clarity and concision. The
page measures each spoken answer: its length, words per minute (about 130 to 160 is
easy to follow on a call), filler words heard, and silences over three seconds. Speech
recognition drops many "um"s and "uh"s, so treat the filler count as a floor.
